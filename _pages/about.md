Hi, I'm Lahiru. I build machine learning systems that see, hear and sense the world: models for video, images,
audio and wearable sensors.

My research centres on **computer vision and multimodal deep learning**, especially monocular depth estimation,
camera-independent 3D perception, and egocentric and wearable sensing. I'm most interested in where these meet
people: healthcare, human activity recognition and assistive systems. Recently I've been working on
**explainable AI for video models**, finding the smallest set of frames that actually drives a model's decision.

I did my PhD in Computer Science at the [University of Virginia](https://engineering.virginia.edu/departments/computer-science),
an MS in Medical Robotics and Image Guided Intervention at Imperial College London, and a BSc in Mechanical Engineering
at the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka. After that I was a research scholar at the University of Iowa, building
multimodal models that combine echocardiogram video with clinical data.

This site is where I keep my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}), [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and
[papers]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}). It's also where I [write]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) and jot down
[stray thoughts]({{ '/thoughts/' | relative_url }}), usually by the scenic route.
