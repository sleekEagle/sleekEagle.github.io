Video models look at dozens of frames, but which ones actually matter? This project develops an explainable AI
method based on **sufficient input subsets**: it isolates the minimal set of frames that is enough for a model to
reach its decision. That helps teams debug and audit video models.

The paper is under review at WACV 2027.
